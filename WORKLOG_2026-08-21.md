## Pricing gaps identified for the next pass

- Tool Create/Edit still exposes a direct Price input that can bypass pricing approval.
- Instruments without an existing price row need an individual **Propose Price Change** action; currently bulk import is the available creation path.
- Instrument details need a consolidated Pricing section showing Standard Price, Latest Purchase Price, supplier, GRN/PO reference, variance and full history.
- Future effective dates are stored but are not yet activated by a scheduled effective-date process.
- Price-history rows need an explicit source such as `STANDARD`, `GRN` or `MANUAL`, plus a PO/GRN reference.
- Bulk-import rejected rows should be downloadable, not only counted.
- A dedicated price-update permission should replace the current general Master/PO permissions.
